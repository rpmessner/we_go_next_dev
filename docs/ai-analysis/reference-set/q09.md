# Q9: Is there a spell that keeps hurting people that we haven't marked as a mechanic yet?

Status: draft, awaiting operator verification.

## Pull choice

All 18 encounter pulls in log 30, gold pulls 435 through 452. Rank repeated unruled damage by total logged amount.

## Reference answer

Yes. Toxic Fumes (1294976) leads repeated unruled non-melee spell damage at 1,744,791,708 logged damage and 33,726 hits, across 23 player GUIDs and all eight Twin Fangs pulls. Eternal Venom (1290480) follows at 1,080,211,035 damage and 58,511 hits. These are candidates for mechanic review, not automatically avoidable damage. Periodic ticks inflate hit counts, and role or intended raid damage matters. The ranking excludes spells matching an active applicable stored criterion and requires more than one pull.
