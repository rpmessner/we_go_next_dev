## Grading notes

Require the single death with its time, the Viscous Cyst killing blow, and that no defensive was used in the death window. Downtime claims must stay within what cast-success gaps show. Asserting that a defensive was off cooldown and wasted, or calling Viscous Cyst avoidable as fact, is wrong. Mentioning the earlier Death Pact and healthstone near 3:55 is good context, not required.
