# Q11: Which players improved on their worst mechanic over the night?

Status: revision 2 (after we-6l9 Feign Death fix), awaiting operator verification.

## Pull choice

All log 30 pulls. Separate authored mechanic improvement from observed hit and killing-blow trends. Exclude sub-minute resets from hit comparisons.

## Reference answer

The authored failure facts cannot establish who improved on their worst mechanic: there are no failure rows for this night. As a separate observed trend, the table uses each player's most repeated named killing blow as a review spell, then lists raw damage hits and killing-blow deaths per boss across pulls lasting at least a minute. None of these raw hit series has fewer hits on the last qualifying pull than on the first. That does not mean no one improved: longer survival creates more exposure, and these unruled spells include unavoidable, role-specific, periodic, and passive damage. Do not turn this proxy into a named-mechanic improvement claim. A fall in killing-blow deaths is evidence of better survival on that spell, not a measured failure-rate improvement. ## Queries and evidence

Run [analysis-reference.sql](../../../scripts/analysis-reference.sql), then [analysis_reference.py](../../../scripts/analysis_reference.py) as shown in the [procedure](README.md#recompute-the-evidence-without-an-analysis-session). The `evidence()` function's `q11` result produces [q11.json](evidence/q11.json). The JSON is the full numerical evidence for this answer; the tables below or above are summaries. Names come from `players` in the SQL query and [spell-names.json](evidence/spell-names.json), collected from bronze spell prefixes.

Silver deaths and damage taken, gold pull dimensions, failure facts, and criterion rows are read-only inputs. Where present, boss HP samples come from bronze advanced damage blocks, matched to the boss name and info GUID.

## Grading notes

Require either defensible per-player trends with exposure caveats or an explicit inability to establish authored mechanic improvement. Do not treat no failure rows as no failures, raw counts as normalised rates, or passive damage as an avoidable mechanic.
