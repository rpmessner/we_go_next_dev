# Q1: Why did Sszorak pull N wipe: what was the first thing that went wrong?

Status: revision 2 (after we-6l9 Feign Death fix), operator-verified 2026-10-30.

## Pull choice

Sszorak pull 5, gold pull 440. It is the longest Sszorak wipe and has the most death events.

## Reference answer

No player dies in the first 155 seconds. The first recorded death is Kahnu, a DPS, at 155.271 seconds. The recorded killing blow is Mutilate (1285999) from Sszorak at 155.248 seconds, for 471,786 logged damage with 15,359 overkill. In the 10 seconds before it, Kahnu takes Ula'tek's Presence pulses of about 50k every 2 seconds and three Viscous Cyst hits of about 31k. This is the earliest demonstrated survival loss, not proof of the first mechanic mistake. Mädz dies next at 171.398 seconds to Tempest (1287083), after several Mutilated Gash ticks and five Tempest hits in about one second. The wipe ends at 411.678 seconds with 25 death events. Deaths recur after the first loss, but the data does not prove that first death caused the eventual wipe. [440, silver.death recap and gold.dim_encounter]

Mädz's earlier `UNIT_DIED` lines at 0:20 and 1:32 carry the Feign Death flag (`unconsciousOnDeath` = 1) and are not deaths. They were excluded by the we-6l9 fix, and Warcraft Logs (report `aW7mTPNrXwLcM2DR`, fight 13) agrees.

## Grading notes

Require the first player (Kahnu), time, blow, and sequence. Naming Mädz at 0:20 or 1:32 as a death is wrong: those are Feign Death lines. Do not accept an invented missed mechanic or a lost tank as the established root cause. A supported causal hypothesis must be marked as inference.
