# Q1: Why did Sszorak pull N wipe: what was the first thing that went wrong?

Status: draft, awaiting operator verification.

## Pull choice

Sszorak pull 5, gold pull 440. It is the longest Sszorak wipe and has the most death events.

## Reference answer

The first recorded death is Mädz, a DPS, at 20.476 seconds. The recorded killing blow is Ula'tek's Presence (1285965) at 20.008 seconds, for 48,969 logged damage. Tempest and Mutilated Gash also hit just before it. This is the earliest demonstrated survival loss, not proof of the first mechanic mistake. Mädz dies again at 91.983 seconds, then Kahnu at 155.271 seconds. The wipe ends at 411.678 seconds with 27 death events. Deaths recur after the first loss, but the data does not prove that first death caused the eventual wipe. [440, silver.death recap and gold.dim_encounter]

## Grading notes

Require the first player, time, blow, and sequence. Do not accept an invented missed mechanic or a lost tank as the established root cause. A supported causal hypothesis must be marked as inference.
