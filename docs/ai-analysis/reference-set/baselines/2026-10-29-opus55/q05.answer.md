# q05: Defensives and consumables at each death, log 30, pulls 435–452

I found about 316 player death events on the night. I checked defensives death by death for 14 of them: every death in the three kill pulls, plus Mädz in pull 436 and the first death in pull 438. For every death I checked whether the player used a healthstone. I could not answer the potion part.

**What the tools could and couldn't show**
- No tool reports whether a cooldown was ready. So "on cooldown" below is my inference from spell timings, and "no cast seen" doesn't mean the defensive was available.
- The defensive check used a fixed list of about 60 major personal and external defensive spell IDs that I chose myself. A defensive outside that list wouldn't show up.
- **Potions are unknown.** I searched for five healing-potion IDs from older expansions, and none appeared in any pull. I don't know this expansion's potion IDs, so this does not show that nobody used a potion.
- **Healthstones are reliable.** Both "Healthstone" (6262) and "Demonic Healthstone" (452930) show up with their names in the log [Sszorak 440, 0:48.9, log_events].
- I listed deaths from the log's death events rather than paging through the full deaths-tool recaps; the two agree for pulls 435 and 436 [deaths; log_events]. Mädz and some others die more than once in a pull. Strongwoman and Sparfox keep casting after their deaths in pull 444, so they were probably resurrected mid-fight (inference); for Mädz the cause is unknown.
