# Sszorak pull 5 (gold pull 440): what went wrong first

Analysis session: 3c365bc9-80ee-4433-993c-0585997861e3

**Short answer:** The first real death was Kahnu (Balance Druid) at 2:35. Kahnu was killed by the boss's second Mutilate, which hits the whole raid. The deaths list shows Mädz dying first, at 0:20 and 1:32, but those two entries look like Feign Death, not real deaths.

## Context
- This was a Heroic wipe with 23 players. It lasted 6:51 and the deaths list has 27 entries [Sszorak pull 5, pull_summary]. I paged through every death page.
- The failures tool returned no rows for this pull [Sszorak pull 5, failures]. That doesn't mean everyone played the mechanics cleanly. It means no tracked mechanic failures are available as evidence.

## Why Mädz's early deaths don't count
- **At 0:20:** Mädz logged an Emergency Salve dispel at 20.464s, then a death 12 ms later at 20.476s. Mädz then cast Cobra Shot at 21.26s, and there was no resurrection event [Sszorak pull 5, 0:20–0:21, log_events].
- **At 1:32:** The same pattern repeated: Emergency Salve at 91.969s, death at 91.983s, then Cobra Shot at 95.456s [Sszorak pull 5, 1:32–1:35, log_events].
- **Inference:** Emergency Salve triggering right before each "death", followed by more casting, fits a Feign Death. However, no cast of Feign Death itself (spell 5384) appears in the log [Sszorak pull 5, log_events].
- **At 2:51:** Mädz died for real. Mädz took a burst of Tempest hits, the last one for 252,706 damage, and cast nothing for the rest of the pull [Sszorak pull 5, 2:51, deaths; log_events].

## The first real problem: Kahnu at 2:35
- **The first Mutilate (0:12)** hit the raid for about 174k–276k per player. Mädz took 265,404 [Sszorak pull 5, 0:12, log_events].
- **The second Mutilate (2:35)** hit much harder: 407k–476k on the non-tank players who appear in that window. Kahnu took 471,786 with nothing absorbed and died with 15,359 overkill. Creepitout took 475,884 and survived [Sszorak pull 5, 2:35, log_events; deaths].
  - **Inference:** Kahnu was lower on health than Creepitout before the hit. I don't have health values, so I can't confirm this.
- **Damage in the 10 seconds before death:**
  - Ula'tek's Presence hit Kahnu for about 50–52k every 2 seconds, and Viscous Cyst ticked for about 31k [Sszorak pull 5, 2:25–2:35, deaths].
  - Kahnu was hit by Viscous Cyst at 1:54, 2:04, 2:12 and 2:23 [Sszorak pull 5, log_events]. That isn't unusual: all 23 players took it, and Kahnu's 24 hits are near the low end (others took up to 65) [Sszorak pull 5, damage_taken].
- **Kahnu's casts from 2:20 to 2:36** were Starfire, two Starsurges, Dash, Moonkin Form, Lunar Eclipse, Fury of Elune, Moonfire and Sunfire [Sszorak pull 5, log_events]. None of these is a defensive cooldown. I can't say whether Barkskin or another defensive was available, so this is not proof of a missed defensive.
