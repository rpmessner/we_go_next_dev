I compared the last Twin Fangs wipe (pull 7, gold 451) with the kill (pull 8, gold 452). Most of the difference is what happened to the tanks. In the wipe the raid was hit three times by a raid-wide Stone Breaker, both tanks died, and the bosses then killed everyone else. In the kill that raid-wide Stone Breaker never happened and only 2 people died.

**Same setup in both pulls.** Both were Heroic with the same 23 players, and they lasted about the same time: 460.4s for the wipe and 445.7s for the kill [Twin Fangs pulls 451/452, pull_summary]. The failures tool returned nothing for either pull, which doesn't prove either was clean [failures 451/452].

## What changed

1. **Deaths fell from 25 to 2** [pull_summary 451/452].
   - In the wipe, 23 different players died; Wispless and Provibria each have two death entries [deaths 451, 3:35 and 7:33/7:38].
   - In the kill, only Kahnu died, at 5:55 (Caustic Deluge 365k and Caustic Globule 306k, then a Toxic Fumes tick), and Bigjím, at 7:04 (Caustic Globule 321k) [deaths 452].

2. **The raid-wide Stone Breaker (spell 1289153, from Ithraz) disappeared.**
   - In the wipe it hit the whole raid three times, at 1:40, 4:26 and 7:19, for about 100k–460k per player each time [log_events 451, 1:40 / 4:26 / 7:19].
   - In the kill it never hit anyone [log_events 452, whole pull; damage_taken 452].
   - **Inference:** something about how the tank-targeted Stone Breaker (spell 1310371) was handled stopped it spreading to the raid. I can't confirm the trigger from this data.

3. **Tank damage was split differently.**
   - Tank Stone Breaker (1310371): in the wipe, Pepsa took 11 hits (3.78M) and Frostygnome took 3. In the kill they split it 9 / 7 [damage_taken 451/452, spell 1310371].
   - Concentrated Spittle: in the wipe, Pepsa took 4 hits (1.68M) and Frostygnome 1. In the kill only Frostygnome was hit, twice [damage_taken spell 1295107].
   - **Inference:** a tank-swap or soak problem. I didn't check any tank-swap assignment.

4. **How the wipe went, in order** [deaths 451]:
   - **3:29–3:35:** Jentle, Wispless and Provibria died with long runs of Eternal Venom ticks in their death logs.
   - **4:50–4:53:** Umbraz, Pixen and Twistedfaith died during overlapping Coiling Ichor and Stir the Depths. This was about 24s after the second raid-wide Stone Breaker.
   - **6:19:** Frostygnome (tank) died; the killing blow was Vexhul's melee.
   - **6:32–6:35:** Mädz, Zuthere and Felthuzad died to Coiling Ichor.
   - **7:07:** Sparfox died after three Caustic Globule hits of about 305k each.
   - **7:16:** Pepsa (tank) died to a 653k Stone Breaker, after Concentrated Spittle hits of 441k, 435k and 340k.
   - **7:18 onward:** with no tanks left, the bosses killed the rest. Vexhul melee hits of 1.0–1.36M killed Saveenó, Omeggon and Zazi. Concentrated Spittle killed Strongwoman, Roarschäch, Dymitrius and Bigjím. Clotted Bolt killed Kahnu and Belprie. The last death was Belprie at 7:39.
   - **Inference:** the first thing to go wrong was the early Eternal Venom deaths, but what actually lost the pull was both tanks dying.

## What did *not* clearly change
- **Stir the Depths:** most players took 13–21 hits in both pulls [damage_taken spell 1292806].
- **Coiling Ichor:** still widely taken in the kill, for example Umbraz took 64 hits (3.33M) [damage_taken 452, spell 1290878].
- **Caustic Globule:** a similar number of hits per player in both pulls [spell 1289201].
- **Toxic Fumes:** higher in the kill (253.7M vs 225.6M) [pull_summary]. **Inference:** that's because more people stayed alive longer, not worse play.

## Not checked

- Battle resurrections. Wispless and Provibria dying twice suggests they were rezzed, but I didn't verify it.
- What actually triggers the raid-wide Stone Breaker.
