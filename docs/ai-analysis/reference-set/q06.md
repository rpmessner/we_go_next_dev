# Q6: Did anyone die through a defensive or potion (the damage was simply too much)?

Status: revision 2 (after we-6l9 Feign Death fix), awaiting operator verification.

## Pull choice

All 18 encounter pulls in log 30, gold pulls 435 through 452. Use a 10-second pre-death cast window and aura evidence.

## Reference answer

Yes, deaths follow observed survival use, but that does not prove unavoidable damage. The evidence selects 56 death events with either a cast in the preceding 10 seconds or an open catalogued aura window at the last damage event or death. Of these, 16 have an aura window open at the last recap hit. Valendara, for example, dies on Twin Fangs pull 4 (448) at 431.195 seconds; Unending Resolve (104773) was applied at 424.146 seconds and remains open at the last recap damage, Eternal Venom for 28,760 at 430.462 seconds. Wispless has Death Pact's aura near a different death, but that aura is a healing-absorb effect, not proof of mitigation. A healthstone or instant-heal potion is a button press, not ongoing protection. The table lists logged damage that still arrived. It cannot establish that healing, movement, or a different defensive could not have saved someone. [435-452, bronze aura windows and casts; silver.death recaps]

Auras are evaluated at the last recap damage as well as at the death notification because death can remove auras before the notification. Equal-timestamp ordering and a missing removal remain uncertain. Aura presence does not quantify absorption or reduction.

## Queries and evidence

Run [analysis-reference.sql](../../../scripts/analysis-reference.sql), then [analysis_reference.py](../../../scripts/analysis_reference.py) as shown in the [procedure](README.md#recompute-the-evidence-without-an-analysis-session). The `evidence()` function's `q06` result produces [q06.json](evidence/q06.json). The JSON is the full numerical evidence for this answer; the tables below or above are summaries. Names come from `players` in the SQL query and [spell-names.json](evidence/spell-names.json), collected from bronze spell prefixes.

`survival()` scans bronze `SPELL_CAST_SUCCESS`, `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED`, `SPELL_AURA_REFRESH`, and `SPELL_AURA_REMOVED` events in file order. It does not read survival or interrupt silver. Each observation carries an original wall-clock timestamp and absolute byte offset. Damage recaps come from silver deaths.

## Grading notes

Require at least a concrete supported death-through-use example and the limits on 'simply too much'. Prefer aura overlap at the damage timestamp to a recent cast alone. Do not call every catalog aura mitigation or assume the effect covered the killing blow when the recap is incomplete.
