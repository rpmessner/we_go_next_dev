# Between-pull method

1. Confirm the log, boss and pull ids with session_context and list_pulls.
2. For a wipe, start with deaths and failures. Order the earliest deviations;
   distinguish the first observed event from an inferred cause of the wipe.
3. Compare like difficulty, roster and fight duration before claiming improvement.
   Separate counts from rates and state missing or stale evidence.
4. Verify interrupt misses with log_events. For defensives, consumables and
   casting gaps, inspect bronze events; absence of a cast does not prove availability.
5. For coaching, pick one evidenced, actionable change per player. Keep it private;
   do not infer intent, assignments or proximity from damage counts.
6. Cite every factual claim. Label causal hypotheses and unsupported claims as
   inference, and say which evidence would resolve them.
